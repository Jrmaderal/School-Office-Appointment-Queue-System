Help & Support

How to use the School Queue System

Getting a Ticket

Go to Get Queue Ticket, choose an office and service, then generate your number. You can hold one active ticket per office.

Tracking Your Queue

Open My Queue to see your position, estimated wait and the number now being served.

Canceling a Ticket

You can cancel while your ticket is still waiting. Once staff call your number it can no longer be canceled.

Offices & Services

See every office, its services and how many students are waiting.

My History

Review your earlier tickets and how they ended.

Notifications

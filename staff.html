Help & Support

How to use the staff dashboard

Call Next

Calls the next waiting student in an office. Use the Call button on a row to call a specific ticket.

Serve, Skip, Transfer

Serve completes a ticket, Skip marks a no-show, and Transfer moves a ticket to another office.

Now Serving Display

Use the Now Serving page as a public display. Switch offices with the dropdown.

History & Export

Queue History shows daily summaries. Export downloads a CSV of the filtered results.

Reports

View served counts, wait times, peak hours and ticket status distribution.

Search & Filter

Filter the queue by office or search by student name, ticket number or service.

Transfer Ticket

Select the destination office for this ticket.
